Energy-Constrained Navigation for Planetary Rovers With Singular Internal Power Source

Under review for IEEE International Conference on Control & Automation(ICCA), 2026

Tianxin Hu*, Weixiang Guo*, Rui Qian, Jiaye Jin, Haoran Zhao, Shenghai Yuan, Lihua Xie

We present an energy-constrained trajectory planning framework for planetary rovers powered by an RTG. The method ensures smooth and dynamically feasible trajectories while strictly satisfying instantaneous power and cumulative energy limits.
