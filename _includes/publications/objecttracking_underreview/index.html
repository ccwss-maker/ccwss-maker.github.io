Interactive Object Tracking on Gait-Parameterized Locomotion Policy

Under review for an international robotics conference

Co-author

Gia-Hung Huynh, Tianxin Hu, Tien-Dat Nguyen, Shenghai Yuan, Thien-Minh Nguyen

We integrate a gait-parameterized locomotion policy as an inner-loop controller with lightweight vision-based ball detection and classical feedback control. The resulting Unitree Go2 system tracks objects in real time while expressing its operational state through trotting, pronking, pacing, and bounding gaits.
