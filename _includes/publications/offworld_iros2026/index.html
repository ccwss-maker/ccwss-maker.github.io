Multi-Agent Off-World Exploration for Sparse Evidence Discovery via Gaussian Belief Mapping and Dual-Domain Coverage

Accepted by IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) , September 2026

Co-author

Zhuoran Qiao, Tianxin Hu, Thien-Minh Nguyen, Shenghai Yuan

We propose a multi-agent informative path planning framework for sparse evidence discovery in hazardous off-world environments. It combines Gaussian-process interest and risk belief maps, trajectory-intent coordination, and dual-domain coverage to improve robustness under biased areas of interest and limited communication.
