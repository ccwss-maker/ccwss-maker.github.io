Selected Ongoing Work in RL Locomotion

This section highlights current hands-on work and technical preparation rather than mature publications or completed achievements.

  • Built and tested locomotion training pipelines for Unitree GO2, including experiments across simulation transfer and early sim-to-real deployment.
  • Reproduced CPG-RL style locomotion methods to better understand how structured priors can improve policy learning and gait stability.
  • Learned and used Isaac Lab for environment setup, policy training, experiment management, and debugging in legged robot tasks.
  • Ran AMP-based training to explore motion-prior-guided locomotion and more natural policy behaviors.
  • Now extending this line toward humanoid locomotion as part of an industry-oriented embodied robotics project.
These efforts reflect my ongoing transition from optimization-based planning and control toward learning-based robot locomotion, and serve as concrete evidence of my current research direction.
