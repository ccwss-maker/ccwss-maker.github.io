Building on Extreme Parkour [1], I extended the locomotion training pipeline for the Unitree Go2 with a focus on stability, training efficiency, and more natural gait behaviour.

① More robust adaptation

Expanded the RMA latent representation to retain richer terrain and dynamics context, improving policy stability during challenging parkour manoeuvres.

② 3.5× faster training

Replaced complex mesh-based training terrain with lightweight box primitives, increasing training throughput by approximately 3.5×.

Parkour policy evaluation in Isaac Sim

Sim-to-real deployment on the Unitree Go2

Together, these changes make the training pipeline substantially more efficient while improving the stability and motion quality of the resulting parkour policy in both simulation and real-world deployment.
