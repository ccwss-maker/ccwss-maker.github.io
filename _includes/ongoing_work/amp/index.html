After briefly exploring the CPG direction, I set aside the interpretability question for now and returned to the original problem: the learned gait still looks unnatural, nothing like real animal locomotion. This led me to discover the Adversarial Motion Priors (AMP) approach [1, 2], which uses reference motion clips captured from real animals or humans as a discriminator signal, guiding the policy to produce stylistically natural gaits without hand-crafting complex reward functions.
